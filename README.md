## 👋 About Me

I am a **Flutter & Dart Mobile, Web, and Desktop Architect** with over 2 years of hands-on experience designing and deploying robust, production-grade applications. My core expertise centers on **GetX State Management**, **Clean Architecture**, **Offline-First SQLite Synchronization**, and responsive UI design across all platforms.

I specialize in building complex enterprise software suites, including **6-app LPG management ecosystems, offline POS systems with ESC/POS thermal printing, e-commerce platforms with payment integrations, logistics fleet tracking, and automated HR & attendance dashboards**. My goal is to build scalable, maintainable, and high-performance software solutions that solve real-world business challenges.
